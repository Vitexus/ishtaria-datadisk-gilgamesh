| 1 | mimma šēru ina nam[āri] | At the very first light of dawn, |
| 2 | išši rēšīšu enkīdu ana pān šamaš ib[akki] | Enkidu lifted up his head, weeping before Šamaš. |
| 3 | ana pān šarūrī ša šamaš ‖ illak[ā dim]ā[šu] | Before the rays of the sun his tears were flowing: |
| 4 | amhurka šamaš ‖ aššum ṣayyādi hābi[li am]ēli | ‘I hereby appeal to you, Šamaš, concerning the hunter, the trapper-man. |
| 5 | ana lāʾiṭi ša lā ušamṣan[ni] ‖ mal[a ibrīya] | *As for* the “shackler”, who did not let me be a match for [my friend,] |
| 6 | ṣayyādu ay inṣâ ‖ mala ibrīšu | may the hunter not be a match for his friend! |
| 7 | nēmelš[u l]ū hariṣ ‖ idīšu muṭṭi | May his income be cut! Diminish his earnings! |
| 8 | lū harṣ[a]t zittašu ina mahr[ī]ka | May his share (of the profits) be cut in your presence!’ |
| 9 | ultu ṣa[yyād]a izzuru ‖ mal[a libbaš]u | After he had cursed the hunter to his [heart’s] content, |
| 10 | u harimta libbašu ‖ arāra u[bl]a | he decided to curse the harlot also. |
| 11 | alkī šamhat ‖ šīmata lušī[mk]i | ‘Come, Šamhat, I will determine a destiny for you, |
| 12 | luzzurki izzira rabâ | I will curse you with a great curse, |
| 13 | hanṭiš harpiš izzirūʾa ‖ liṭhû ana kâši | my curses shall afflict you swiftly and soon! |
| 14 | ē tēpušī bīt lalêki | May you not find a household to delight in! |
| 15 | ē tušbī ina kussî ša ardāti | may you not sit down in the young women’s chair! |
| 16 | ṣubātki! damqa ‖ qaqqaru lišehhi | May the ground defile your fine-looking garment! |
| 17 | lubār isinnāti šakrum ‖ ina tu[r]buʾi liballil | May the drunkard smear with dust your festive gown! |
| 18 | ē taršî bīt unâti | May you never acquire a house with utensils and pots, |
| 19 | bītki lū [hašt]um ša [p]a[hā]ri | [for your home shall serve] a potter’s *clay pit*! |
| 20 | ... [...] lū ... ella ay irši | She shall have neither [bedroom, nor family shrine, nor] holy [*hearth*!] |
| 21 | ... [... p]aššūru šumuh nišī bāltum ay innadi ina bītīki? | [No bed, chair or] table, that people take pride in, shall be found in your chamber! |
| 22 | d[i]nnût lalêki lū dakkan ... [...] | The couch you delight in shall be a hard bench! |
| 23 | išpallurtī pahārim lū mūšabūk[i] | May the crossroads of the potter be where you sit! |
| 24 | hurbā[t]um lū mayyālak[i] | May the ruined houses be where you bed down! |
| 25 | ṣilli dūri lū manzāzūk[i] | May the lee of the city wall be where you stand! |
| 26 | ešēgu baltu ‖ liqellipū šēp[īki] | May briar and thorn skin [your] feet! |
| 27 | [šakr]u u ṣamû ‖ limha<ṣū> lē[tki] | May drunk and sober strike [your] cheek! |
| 28 | [...] ... sūqi altammaki lidepp[ir] | May [the *rabble*] *of* the street congregate in your brothel! |
| 29 | [... l]iššakin ṣāl[tum] | [*In your* *tavern*] may there be fights! |
| 30 | [... bē]lti dīni! ‖ el[īki lil]si | ‘[*Wives*] *shall* be plaintiff, and bring claims against [you!] |
| 31 | [ūr bītīki ‖ ay isē]r i[tinnu] | [May the] builder [not plaster *the roof of your house*!] |
| 32 | [ina ... l]irbiṣū ka[lbū] | [*In your bed-chamber* may] there lie [*wild*] *dogs*! |
| 33 | [... ay išš]akin q[erītum] | [Never in your house shall banquet] take place! |
| 34 | [...] ... [...] | […] … […] |
| 35 | [...] ... [...] | […] … […] |
| 36 | [...] ... tarīmt[aki?] | […] … [*your*] *present.* |
| 37 | [ša lubār taki]lti lū ... [...] | […] purple [raiment] may …! |
| 38 | [... sūn]i šuhhû lū nidin ... | May a defiled […] be the gift of …! |
| 39 | [ašsum yâ]ši ‖ ella tušemṭinni | [Because] you made me weak, me who was pure! |
| 40 | [u yâš]i ella ‖ tušemṭinni ina ṣērīya | [And me] who was pure, you made me weak when I was in the wild.’ |
| 41 | [šamaš išme] ‖ zikir pîšu | [Šamaš heard] what he had spoken, |
| 42 | [ultu ullânum-ma tuk]ku ‖ ultu šamê išassašum-ma | [straight away] a voice cried to him from the heavens: |
| 43 | [...] ṣayyāda ‖ hābila amēla | [‘…] hunter, trapper-man, |
| 44 | [...] ... lū damqat | […] … she/it should be lovely. |
| 45 | ... [... en]kīdu būla ... | […] Enkidu, the animals…, |
| 46 | ... [... šam]hatum ... | May [… *the*] *prostitute*…. |
| 47 | ... [...] ... [...] kī telli | You […] … *how will you go up*?’ |
| 48 | alkī šamh[at ‖ šīmata lušīm]ki | ‘Come, Šamḫat, [I shall determine a destiny for] you, |
| 49 | pīya ša izz[urū]ki ‖ l[itūr] lik[ru]bki | my mouth that cursed you shall return and bless you: |
| 50 | šakkanakkū u r[ub]û lirāmūki | May governors and noblemen love you, |
| 51 | ša ištēn bēr ‖ limhaṣ šaparšu | may he who is one league (distant) slap his thigh! |
| 52 | ša šina bēr ‖ linessisa qimmassu | May he who is two leagues (distant) shake out his locks, |
| 53 | ay iklâkki rēdû ‖ meserrašu lipṭurki | may no soldier be slow to undo his belt for you! |
| 54 | liddinki ṣupra u kišāda | May he give you fingernail and necklace, |
| 55 | anṣabtū turturri ‖ limellâ uznīka | With earrings of gold he shall deck your ears! |
| 56 | ana eṭli ša kunnu kunūnūšu ‖ išpi[kkūšu ša]pkū | to a man whose household is well off, whose storage [bins are heaped] high! |
| 57 | ištar lēʾât ilī lišēribki | May Ištar, most able of the gods, send you in |
| 58 | aššumīka linnezib ummi sebetti hīrtu | On your account may the first wife be deserted, the mother of seven!’ |
| 59 | gilgāmeš ašib maharšu | Gilgameš was sitting before him. |
| 60 | uš[tabba]l mimmu kabtatīšu | Mulling over what was on his mind, |
| 61 | iqabbâššu ana šâšu | (Enkidu) spoke to him: |
| 62 | m[i]mmu ibrī ‖ ina šunat mušītīya | ‘All (that I saw), my friend, in the dream of this night: |
| 63 | i[lsû]-ma šamû ‖ qaqqaru īpul | the heavens thundered and the earth gave echo, |
| 64 | ina birīšunu azzaz anāku | with me standing (there) between them. |
| 65 | ištēn eṭlu ‖ ukkulū pānūšu | There was a man, his expression was grim, |
| 66 | ana ša anzî nāʾer ‖ pānūšu mašlū | his face was like a ravening Anzû bird. |
| 67 | ritti nēši ritt[āšu] | [His] hands were a lion’s paws, |
| 68 | ṣupur erî ṣupr[āšu] | [his] claws were an eagle’s talons. |
| 69 | iṣbat qimmatī yāši uden[ni]n? | He seized me by the hair and *overpowered me*.’ |
