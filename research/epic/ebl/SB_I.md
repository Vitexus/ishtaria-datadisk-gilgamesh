| 1 | [ša nagba īmuru ‖ i]šdī māti | [He who saw the Deep, the] foundation of the country, |
| 2 | [alkakāti īd]û ‖ kalāma hass[u] | [who knew all ways], was wise in everything! |
| 3 | [gilgāmeš ša n]agba īmuru ‖ išdī mā[ti] | [Gilgameš, who] saw the Deep, the foundation of the country, |

| 5 | [ihī]ṭ-ma mithāriš par[akkī] | [He explored] everywhere the seats [of power,] |
| 6 | [naph]ar nēmeqi ‖ ša kalāmi ī[de] | he [knew] the totality of wisdom about everything. |
| 7 | [ni]ṣirta īmur-ma ‖ katimta ipte | He saw the secret and uncovered the hidden, |
| 8 | ubla ṭēma ‖ ša lām abūbi | he brought back a message from the antediluvian age. |
| 9 | [u]rha rūqta illikam-ma ‖ anih u šupšuh | He came a distant road and was weary but granted rest, |
| 10 | [šak]in ina narî ‖ kalu mānahti | all (his) labours [were] set down on a tablet of stone. |
| 11 | uppiš dūra ‖ ša uruk supūri | He built the wall of Uruk-the-Sheepfold, |
| 12 | ša ayakki qudduši ‖ šutummi elli | of holy Eanna, the pure storehouse. |
| 13 | amur dūršu ‖ ša kīma qê nipš[u] | See its wall which is like a *strand of wool*, |
| 14 | itaplas samītašu ‖ ša lā umaššalu mamma | view its parapet which nobody can replicate! |
| 15 | ṣabat-ma simmilta ‖ ša ultu ullânu | Take the stairway that has been there since ancient times, |
| 16 | qitrub ana ayakki ‖ šubat ištar | and draw near to Eanna, the seat of Ištar, |
| 17 | ša šarru arkû ‖ lā umaššalu mamma | that no later king can ever replicate. |
| 18 | elī-ma ina muhhi dūri ‖ ša uruk itallak | Go up on to the wall of Uruk and walk around, |
| 19 | temmēna hīṭ-ma ‖ libitta ṣubbi | survey the foundation platform, inspect the brickwork! |
| 20 | šumma libittašu lā agurrat | (See) if its brickwork is not kiln-fired brick, |
| 21 | u uššīšu lā iddû ‖ sebet muntalkū | and if the Seven Sages did not lay its foundations! |
| 22 | [šār] ālu šār kirâtu šār essû pitir bīt ištar | [One *šār* is] city, one *šār* date-grove, one *šār* is clay-pit, half a *šār* the temple of Ištar: |
| 23 | šalāšat šār u pitir ‖ uruk tamšīhu | [three *šār*] and a half, Uruk, (its) measurement. |

| 25 | [puṭṭe]r hargallīšu ša siparri | [release] its clasps of bronze! |
