Noto Sans Cuneiform (subset)
============================

Bundled as: noto-sans-cuneiform-numerals.ttf — the Noto Sans Cuneiform font of the Noto project,
reduced to the two cuneiform number signs the memorials use (U+12079 DISH, U+1230B U) and a space
(fontTools subsetter, no other change; see tools/subset_cuneiform_font.sh).
